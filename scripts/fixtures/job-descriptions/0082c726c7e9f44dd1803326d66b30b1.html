Join our team as a Territory Manager within our Emergency Care division in Melbourne, Victoria.

About Stryker

Stryker is one of the world’s leading medical technology companies, and together with our customers, we are driven to make healthcare better.

We offer innovative products and services in Medical and Surgical, Neurotechnology, Orthopaedics, and Spine that help improve patient and healthcare outcomes. Alongside our customers around the world, Stryker impacts more than 100 million patients annually.

The Role

As a Territory Manager, you will take full ownership of your territory, managing and growing our Emergency Care portfolio across metro Melbourne and Tasmania. This role focuses on delivering industry-leading defibrillation and patient monitoring technologies used in emergency departments, intensive care units, cardiac catheter labs, and acute care environments.

You'll partner closely with emergency clinicians, cardiac teams, nurses, biomedical engineers, and hospital stakeholders to deliver solutions that support high-performance resuscitation, improve clinical outcomes, and drive standardisation within hospitals.

You'll be part of a high-performing, collaborative team that's passionate about innovation and making a difference in critical moments of care. Every day, you'll be contributing to our mission to make healthcare better.

This role is primarily focused on Melbourne, with occasional travel to Tasmania to support key customers and growth opportunities across the territory.
